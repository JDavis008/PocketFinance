Connect to your PocketFinance

On your PC, open PocketFinance → Settings → Connect your phone and type the address it shows — it looks like http://192.168.1.20:48140.

Can't connect?
  • Your PC is on and PocketFinance Server is running (it starts when you sign in to Windows).
  • Phone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi, and Allow my Wi-Fi is on in that Settings card.
  • Away from home: install the free Tailscale app on the PC and phone and use the Tailscale address.
